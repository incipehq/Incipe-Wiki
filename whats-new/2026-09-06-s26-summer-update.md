This release reshapes the INCIPE app around a calmer starting point, a more capable project workspace, and a single consistent interface system.

## Landing page and Projects rail

The app now opens to a focused landing page with **New Project**, **Open Project**, and recent work — no seeded demos on first run.

Projects are named from their folder contents, and the sample projects now live in the **State Inspector**.

The **Projects** rail is slimmer at 220px. Hover to peek, click to dock, and search chats as well as folders.

Chats now live under their project in one file tree. New chats remain drafts until their first message, and unfiled chats can be dragged into a project.

Folder colours use the shared eight accent palettes, the whole project row opens its disclosure, and the rail shares one empty state and one scroll region.

## Chat and AI companion

A new mascot blob follows the pointer, looks around while idle, and reacts to clicks and AI settings. It now moves between the empty state and composer instead of rendering twice.

The blob peeks from behind the chat box, replies are signed by **Incipe AI**, and status tags have become one concise state word.

**Rewind** is now a single in-conversation editor launched from a message menu. Your-message pencil becomes a copy action, and proposed plans always render as markdown.

The composer sends with an upward arrow, while its mode, model, and thinking menus open on press and are pinned to each chat.

## Workspace, board and panels

The header has one tab strip, tighter toolbar spacing, clearer **Board** controls, and matching USB and OTA push actions.

The board dock is always visible. Offline, it shows the USB-C connection artwork; on connection it grows smoothly from its waiting state.

**Artifacts** begin with **Library**, **Console** tabs behave like selected rows, and the editor, skills list, and file tree have been simplified around shared controls.

## Settings, onboarding and interface system

**Day/Night** and **Comfy/Outline** use the shared segmented control. Settings now use centred content, a meaningful active state, and compact actions.

Onboarding is centred and sequenced in reading order. The brand mark becomes the blob at **Hi, I'm INCIPE**, with clearer survey and theme choices.

Dialogs, menus, notices and form controls now share a softer, more intentional recipe with native selects replaced by app-owned menus.

Type has been consolidated to two UI sizes plus caps, every surface uses a 6px radius and hairline edges, and the new eight-palette accent system keeps states clear without overusing outlines.

## Reliability fixes

This update also resolves rail peek behaviour, lost chat-history mapping, chat ordering, unfiled-chat selection, duplicate replay approvals, mascot production visibility and panel-resize flicker — along with dozens of small layout, translation, clipping and interaction refinements.
