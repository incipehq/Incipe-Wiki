*Next week another class borrows the monitor. They will want to know what the warnings mean and how to change the threshold. Have Incipe write the manual, and keep it.*

## Ask for a document

Send:

> Write a one-page README for the climate monitor: what to plug where, what each serial line means, and how to change the warning temperature.

The reply ends with an **artifact chip**: an icon, the title and an id. Click it to open the document in **Artifacts**.

## The Artifacts panel

Click **Artifacts** in the top bar.

```screen
image: /app-guide/panel-artifacts.jpg

49.9, 11.1 | Search artifacts | Find one by title.
3.7, 14.8 | Filters | All, Code, Doc, Table, Diagram.
49.9, 62.5 | Cards | Click one to preview it.
```

In **Preview**:

- **Back to library** returns to the list.
- The version menu (**v1**, **v2**…) shows earlier versions when Incipe revised it.
- **Copy to clipboard** and **Download** take it out of the app, to print or hand in.

Ask for changes in the chat: *"Add a section on where to place the monitor."* Incipe saves a new version; the old one stays in the menu.

## You can now

- Get documents, plans and diagrams from Incipe and keep them with the project.
- Compare versions and download a copy.

**Next:** before the final build, set the app up the way you like it.
