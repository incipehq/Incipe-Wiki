*Room 204 gets stuffy by the afternoon, and nobody notices until heads start to drop. Over the next 15 steps you will build a **classroom climate monitor**: an INCIPE Board that reads temperature and humidity, graphs them, tells you when to open a window, and takes updates over Wi-Fi from your desk. It starts with the app.*

## Install the app

1. Download **INCIPE Workspace for Mac** from the link your school or Incipe sent you.
2. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Incipe** into **Applications**.
3. Open **Incipe** from Applications.

INCIPE Workspace runs on macOS. It updates itself: when a new version is ready, a notice offers **Update now** (see [Notices, tips and help](/wiki/app-help)).

## Open a folder for the project

The app opens on the landing page every time. Everything a project has, its code, its chats and its board settings, lives in one folder.

1. Choose **New project** (⌘N).
2. In the picker, make a new empty folder called `climate-reader` and choose **Start here**.

The workspace opens on that folder. Next time, it is listed under **Recent projects**.

## Sign in

Your school's administrator created your account. There is no sign-up.

1. Click **Switch project** (the pane icon, top left) to show the project pane.
2. At the bottom of the pane, click **Sign in**.
3. Enter the **Email** and **Password** you were given, then **Sign in**.

Your name now shows at the bottom of the pane. Until you sign in, **Verify**, **Flash** and the AI stay switched off.

## You can now

- Open INCIPE Workspace and start a project from a folder.
- Sign in with your school account.
