*You will spend a lot of hours in this window. Make it easy on the eyes.*

## Open Settings

Click your name at the bottom of the project pane (**Account menu**) › **Settings**. **Back to workspace** (top left) returns you.

## Appearance

```screen
image: /app-guide/settings-appearance.jpg
alt: Settings, Appearance section
caption: Your app may look slightly different.
---
5.1, 24.7 | Appearance | Settings sections are listed on the left.
81.1, 17.9 | Interface font | Archivo, Inter, Outfit or Helvetica Neue. Code and buttons stay in JetBrains Mono.
74.5, 27.3 | Accent colour | One accent colour for the whole app. Status colours never change.
81.1, 36.8 | Language | English, 繁體中文, 简体中文 or 日本語.
79.3, 56.6 | Chrome | Comfy fills secondary buttons; Outline draws a line around them.
82.5, 66 | Reduced motion | Follows your Mac's setting. Turn it on in macOS to stop animations.
82, 74.4 | RESET | Back to the default look. Projects are untouched.
```

The language changes the app's own labels. Code, logs, the board's output and Incipe's replies stay as they are.

## Other sections

| Section | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your signed-in email, and **LOG OUT** |
| **Plan & usage** | Your plan and credits, when your account has one |
| **Skills** | The skills Incipe can load. Click one to open it in the Skills panel |

## You can now

- Change the font, accent colour, language and look.
- Find your account and log out.
