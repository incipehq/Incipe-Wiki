## The four regions

| Region | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Top bar** | Build and upload (**Verify**, **Flash**), check the board, open panels |
| **Project pane** | Switch projects, open or start chats, get help, open your account |
| **Chat** | Talk to Incipe, the AI that writes and fixes your firmware |
| **Side panel** | Look closer: the code, the serial monitor, the board in 3D, documents |

Panels resize by dragging their edge. **Expand panel** (top right of a panel) gives it the whole width; **Close panel** (×) puts the chat back.
